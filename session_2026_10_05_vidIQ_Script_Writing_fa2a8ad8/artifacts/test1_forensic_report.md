## The Real Question: Can This Script Work?

**As-is? No.** It needs surgery. Specifically:

1. **Delete the Introduction section entirely** — go straight from hook to Fire
2. **Inject personality into the narration voice** — add direct address, rhetorical questions, "let me show you why this matters" moments
3. **Add back the missing wow-moments** — hibernation theory, bone sunglasses, cold trap doorway, tallow
4. **Add at least one humor beat** — doesn't have to be the reference's exact style, but something to break the academic monotony
5. **Rewrite Section 6 (Society)** with concrete examples instead of abstract concepts
6. **Kill the generic CTA** — end on a thought-provoking note or weave the subscribe into a natural closer

**After surgery? Yes, it could work.** The structural bones are solid. It just needs a voice transplant and some missing details put back in.
