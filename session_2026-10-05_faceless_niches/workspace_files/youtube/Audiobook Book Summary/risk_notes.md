# Risk Notes — Audiobook / Book Summary Channel
# Created: Sept 2026

## YouTube Policy Risks (2025-2026)

### 1. Inauthentic Content Policy (Replaced "Repetitious Content" — July 2025)
- YouTube now flags content created strictly to harvest views without meaningful human creative input
- Covers: rigid template workflows, mass-produced AI slop, no original analysis
- Stock footage + generic voiceover = NOT transformative
- **Your risk level:** MEDIUM — AI voice + captions on images is very simple. Must have strong script quality and human QC to pass.

### 2. Mandatory AI Disclosure
- Must check "Altered or Synthetic Content" box for AI voice, AI images, AI video
- YouTube runs multimodal classifiers to detect undisclosed AI content
- Repeated non-disclosure = strikes, algorithmic suppression, YPP removal
- **Your risk level:** LOW — just check the box every time. Zero cost.

### 3. Feb 1, 2027 — Monetization Threshold Doubles
- Watch hours: 4,000 → 8,000 in previous 365 days
- Shorts views: 10M → 20M in previous 90 days
- Subscribers: stays at 1,000
- Grandfathering: existing YPP partners before Feb 1, 2027 stay in
- **Your risk level:** HIGH — if this channel is a secondary project, you likely won't hit threshold before deadline. Will face 8,000-hour requirement.

### 4. Algorithm Satisfaction Weighting
- YouTube now weights satisfaction signals (post-watch surveys, "Not Interested" rates, repeat viewership)
- Frame-by-frame AI analysis of pacing, visual redundancy, tone
- Generic stock loops and repetitive AI pacing get suppressed
- **Your risk level:** MEDIUM — long-form audiobook content naturally has high watch time if content is good. But AI pacing could be detected.

### 5. Market Saturation
- AI tools lowered barrier to zero. Millions of low-quality faceless channels flooded every niche 2023-2025
- Impression pools diluted, CPMs plummeted for generic faceless content
- Advertisers blacklist channels showing signs of automated content
- **Your risk level:** MEDIUM — audiobook niche is crowded. Need differentiation.

## Copyright Risks

### Public Domain Books
- **Risk:** ZERO — free to narrate, summarize, or read in full
- Examples: Pride and Prejudice, Art of War, Meditations, 1984 (depends on country)

### Copyrighted Bestsellers (Atomic Habits, Rich Dad Poor Dad, etc.)
- **Full narration / reading aloud:** HIGH RISK — essentially pirating the audiobook
- **Chapter-by-chapter summary:** MEDIUM RISK — gray area, some channels do it
- **Summary with commentary/analysis:** LOWER RISK — transformative use argument
- **Key insight:** No major book summary channels have been mass-struck yet, but individual copyright holders CAN file claims
- **Safe boundary:** Summarize + add your own commentary/analysis. Never read the book word-for-word.

## Mitigation Strategies

### Must-Do (Non-Negotiable)
1. **Script humanization** — AI draft → YOU rewrite hook + transitions → /humanizer → /ai-writing-gaps
2. **AI disclosure checkbox** — ALWAYS check. Every upload. Never skip.
3. **Voice quality** — Use premium AI voice or clone YOUR voice. Never use generic robotic TTS.
4. **Appeal dossier** — Save script drafts, project files, edit history from Day 1

### Should-Do (Strongly Recommended)
5. **Differentiation** — Add your own commentary/analysis to every summary. Don't just summarize.
6. **Visual fingerprint** — Consistent thumbnail style, color palette, branding
7. **Revenue diversification** — Don't depend 100% on AdSense. Add book affiliate links.
8. **Multi-platform** — Distribute audio to Spotify, Apple Podcasts

### Nice-to-Have
9. **Playlist strategy** — Group by genre/author for session binge loops
10. **Community building** — Discord or comment engagement for repeat viewership signals
