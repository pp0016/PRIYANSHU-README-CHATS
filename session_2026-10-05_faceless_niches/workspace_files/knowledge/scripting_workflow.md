# Scripting Workflow (ENFORCED — Non-Negotiable)

**Added:** 2026-09-22
**Applies to:** ALL channels, ALL videos, ANY time a script is being written.

## Priyanshu's Scripting Method (The "Aggregation + Crazy Story" Pipeline)

Whenever a script is being created, this exact pipeline must be followed to ensure high retention and bypass basic AI detection, while relying on Opus 4.6 for elite writing quality.

### Step 1: Competitor Aggregation
- Extract the transcripts from **5 to 10 top-performing competitor videos** on the exact same topic.
- Do NOT just summarize one video. Pull from the entire top competitive landscape.

### Step 2: AI Line Extraction
- Feed the 5-10 transcripts to the AI (Opus 4.6).
- Instruct the AI to extract only the **most engaging, high-retention lines, hooks, and core arguments** from all the transcripts combined.
- Discard the fluff. Keep only what actually worked for competitors.

### Step 3: Structural Framing
- Take those extracted, high-performing lines and drop them into a proven script writing structure/format.
- Have Opus 4.6 write the connecting lines, transitions, and pacing to ensure the flow is seamless and highly engaging.

### Step 4: The "Crazy Example Story" (The Originality Injector)
- **MANDATORY:** You must inject a completely original "crazy example story," personal take, or bizarre historical anecdote related to the book/topic.
- This story must NOT be in the competitor videos. 
- **Why this matters:** This is the human element that forces the script to be legally and algorithmically transformative. It ensures the video isn't just a clone, but a unique piece of content.

### Step 5: Final Polish
- Run the assembled script through `/humanizer` to kill any remaining AI tells (em dashes, predictable vocabulary).
- Run through `/ai-writing-gaps` to check pacing and structural contrast.
- Ensure the final output does not sound like a Wikipedia summary, but rather a top-tier, human-written essay.
